# Design engineering

Every animation needs a reason, trigger, duration, easing, interruption behavior, and exit. Prefer instant feedback for direct manipulation, subtle emphasis for state change, and no motion when it adds noise. Check focus, hover, disabled, error, and reduced-motion states.
